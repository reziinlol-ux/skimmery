Terms of Service

These terms explain account access, credits, orders, and the rules that apply when the service is available.

Eligibility and platform rules

You must follow the rules for Google, Steam, Gorilla Tag, and any other service connected to an account. Steam's Subscriber Agreement describes accounts as personal and limits transfers. The marketplace does not authorize conduct those services prohibit. Review the current Steam Subscriber Agreement and Gorilla Tag Terms of Service.

Listings and odds

Displayed listing percentages are supplied by the marketplace operator and are not independently audited. The 2022 and 2023 account percentages, value tiers, and OVR Advanced Settings percentages describe separate attributes and may not add up to 100%. No particular account or attribute is guaranteed.

Credits and tips

Credits are an internal unit. The displayed conversion is an estimate and does not make credits redeemable for cash. Sending a tip is final after it is confirmed. Credit additions and account orders are subject to availability and any payment terms shown at checkout.

Account orders and support

Account orders are unavailable until the operator confirms that the listed inventory can be transferred lawfully and securely. Never share your Google password or verification codes with the marketplace. Support contact details must be published by the operator before public sales begin.

Changes and contact

The operator may update listings, features, and these terms. Before public launch, the operator must add its legal name, contact details, governing-law information, and any required consumer notices here.

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information the marketplace is designed to handle when connected to its hosted service.

Information collected

When you sign in with Google, the service receives your Google account identifier, verified email address, and profile image. It also stores the username you choose, credit balance and ledger entries, tips, order status, notifications, and security session records.

How information is used

Account information is used to sign you in, reserve your username, show your balance and activity, route tips, prevent fraud, and provide support. The service does not need your Google password and does not receive it through Google sign-in.

Storage and service providers

Marketplace records are intended to be stored in a private PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway. Google handles sign-in. Railway and Google process data under their own terms. The app uses session cookies to keep you signed in. The service does not sell profile or account information.

Retention and security

Session records expire after 30 days and can be revoked at sign-out. Account and ledger records need a published deletion and retention schedule before public launch. Passwords and Google access tokens are not stored by the marketplace. Do not submit payment card details in this app.

Your choices and contact

You can sign out from the profile menu. Requests to access or delete account information must be handled by the operator. Before public launch, the operator must publish a contact address, legal identity, retention schedule, and the privacy rights that apply in the places it serves.
